1.) ところ

"ところ" means place but in this context is used to indicate a temporal position.

1.1) V(dict.) ところです - is about to start

Indicates that something is about to start:

ひるごはんは　もう　たべましたか？ …いいえ、これから　たべるところです。

Have you had lunch yet? ...No, I'm going to have it now.

#
1.2) Vて　いる　ところです - is being done now

Indicates that an action is being done now:

いま　しらべて　いる　ところです。

#
1.3) Vた　ところです - has just finished ~

Indicates that an action has just been completed:

ミラーさんは　たったいま　かえった　ところです。

Mr. Miller has just left.

2.) Vた　ばかりです - happened recently

Indicates that not much time has passed since an action or event has occurred:

さっき　ひるごはんを　たべた　ばかりです。

I had lunch only a while ago.

3.) はずです I'm sure that...

Indicates that the speaker is convinced/quite sure of what is stated:

ミラーさんは　きょう　くるでしょうか？

くる　はずですよ。　きのう　でんわが　ありましたから。

Do you think Mr. Miller will come today?

I'm sure he'll come. I received a call from him yesterday

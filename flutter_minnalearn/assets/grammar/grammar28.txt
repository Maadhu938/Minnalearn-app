1) V1ます-form ながら V2

This pattern indicates performing an action V1 while simultaneously performing another action V2(V2 being the main action).

おんがくを　ききながら　しょくじします。

I listen to music while eating.

はたらきながら　にほんごを　べんきょうします。

I'm working while studying Japanese.

2) Vて-form います

As well as indicating what someone is doing now, this can also indicate a habitual behaviour. Such behaviour in the past is indicated by いました.

まいあさ　ジョッギングを　して　います。

I go jogging every morning.

こどものとき、まいばん　８じに　ねて　いました。

When I was a child, I used to go to bed at eight every evening.

#
3.1) Plain form-し, plain form-し, ~

This pattern is used when mentioning two or more similar things about a topic.

すずきさんは　ピアノも　ひけるし、うたも　うたえるし、だんすも　できます。

Mr. Suzuki can play the piano, sing and dance.

Since this pattern expresses the speaker's desire to mention more than just one thing about the topic, も is often used. それに can also be used to make this even clearer:

たなかさんは　まじめだし、ちゅうごくごも　じょうずだし、それに　けいけんも　あります。

Mr. Tanaka is serious, speaks good Chinese, and also has experience.

#
3.2) This pattern can also be used to give reasons:

ここは　ねだんも　やすいし、さかなも　あたらしし、よく　たべに　きます。

The prices are low here and the fish is fresh, so I often come here to eat.

The final し can be replaced by から in it's "because" sense:

どうして　アニメが　すきなんですか。

はなしも　おもしろいし、おんがくも　すてきですから。

Why do you like Anime?

Because the stories are interesting and I like the music.

#
4)　それで - that's why

Indicates that what is about to be said follows from what has just been said:

しょうらい　しょうせつかに　なりたいです。それで　いまは　アルバイトを　しながら　しょうせつを　かいて　います。

I want to be a novelist, so now I'm writing a novel and working part-time while doing so.

ここは　コーヒーも　おいしいし、しょくじも　できるし。　

~ それで　にんきが　あるんですね。

The coffee's good here, and you can also have food.

~ So that's why it's popular, isn't it?

1.) looks like

1.1) Usage with Vます-form

This pattern is used to presuppose an occurrence. いまにも, もうすぐ, これから, etc., may be used to refer to time the occurrence might take place.

いまにも　あめが　ふりそうです。

It looks like it will rain any moment.

シャンプーが　なくなりそうです。

It looks like we are running out of shampoo.

#
1.2) Usage with adjectives

Used to express that something seems a certain way, but isn't confirmed.

この　りょうりは　からそうです。

This dish looks spicy.

この　つくえは　じょうぶそうです。

This desk looks durable.

2.) Vて-form きます　- "to go somewhere, do something and come back"

ちょっと　たばこを　かって　きます。

I'm going out to buy some cigarettes.

Special forms are "いって　きます" and "でかけて　きます", meaning "to go somewhere and return" and "to go out and return":

ゆうびんきょくへ　いって　きます。

I'm going to the post office.

ちょっと　でかけて　きます。

I'm going outside for a minute.

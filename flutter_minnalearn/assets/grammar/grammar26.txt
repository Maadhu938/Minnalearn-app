1) ～んです

～んです　is an expression used to explain causes, reasons, grounds, etc., strongly. ～んです is used in spoken language, while ～のです is used in written language. ～んです is used in the following ways.

1.1 ～んですか

• to confirm the reason for something.

わたなべさんは　ときどき　おおさかべんを　つかいますね。おおさかに　すんで　いたんですか

Ms. Watanabe, you sometimes speak Osaka dialect. Have you lived in Osaka?

• to ask for information about what one has seen or heard.

おもしろい　デザインの　くつですね。　どこで　かったんですか。

This shoe has an interesting design. Where did you buy it?

• to ask for the reason of something

どうして　おくれたんですか。

Why were you late?

• when asking for an explanation

どうしたんですか？

What's the matter?

#
1.2 ～んです

•　to state a resaon

バスが　こなかったんです。

(I was late) Because the bus didn't come.

#
1.3 ～んですが

• Used to introduce a topic, followed by a request.

にほんごで　てがみを　かいたんですが、ちょっと　みて　いただけませんか。

I wrote a letter in Japanese, could you do me the favor of taking a look?

#
2) Vて いただけませんか　Would you please do me the favor of..

いい　せんせいを　しょうかいして　いただけませんか。

Could you introduce me to a good teacher?

#
3) Interrogative Vたら いいですか

What/When/Where shall I.. ?

～たら　いいですか　is used when asking for advice/instructions what to do.

どこで　カメラを　かったら　いいですか。

Where should I buy a camera?

#
4) は

You learned in lesson 10 and 17 that subjects and also objects indicated by を can become topics and be highlighted by は. Objects indicated by が can also be highlighted by は.

1.) か

A question with an interrogative is used as a component of a sentence in this pattern:

JL107は　なんじに　とうちゃくするか、しらべて　ください。

Please check what time JL107 will arrive.

けっこんの　おいわいは　なにが　いいか、はなして　います。

We are talking about what would be good as a wedding present.

2.) か　どうか

This pattern can be translated as "whether (or not)". It uses a question without an interrogative as a component of a sentence:

その　はなし　ほんとうか　どうか、わかりません。

I don't know whether this story is true.

まちがいが　ないか　どうか、しらべて　ください。

Please check whether there are no mistakes.

3.) Vて-form みます "Try doing something"

この　ズボンを　はいて　みても　いいですか？

May I try (putting) these trousers on?

もう　いちど　かんがえて　みます。

I'll think it over again.

4.) い-adj ～さ ~ness

Transforms the adjective into a noun:

たかい→たかさ(height)

はやい→はやさ(speed)

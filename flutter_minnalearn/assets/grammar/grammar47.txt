1.) plain form そうです　　　I hear that..

This is an expression for conveying information that you have obtained from another source. The source CAN be indicated by ～に　よると(according to).

てんきようほうに　よると、あしたは　さむく　なるそうです。

According to the weather report, it will get cold tomorrow.

バリとうは　とても　きれいだそうです。

I hear that Bali is very beautiful.

Note: Difference to ます-form そうです

あめが　ふりそうです。　It looks like rain.

あめが　ふるそうです。　I heard that it will rain.

2.) ～ようです It seems that..

～ようです conveys the speaker's conjecture. It is sometimes accompanied by どうま at the beginning of the sentence to show that the speaker is uncertain about what is stated.

ひたが　おおい　あつまって　いますね。じこのようでえすね。

There's a big crowd is gathered over there. It looks like there has been an accident.

せきも　でるし、あたまも　いたい。どうも　かぜを　ひいたようだ。

I have a cough and a headache. It looks like I've caught a cold.

Note: Difference between ～そうです and ～ようです

ミラーさんは　いそがしそうです。

Mr. Miller seems busy.

ミラーさんは　いそがしようです。

It seems that Mr. Miller is busy.

3.) こえ/おと/におい/あじ が　します

This pattern is used to describe a phenomenon that is perceivable by the senses:

へんな　においが　しますね。

It smells strange in here.

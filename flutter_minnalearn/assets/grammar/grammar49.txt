1) そんけいご(polite expressions)

そんけいご is used to describe the listener or the person referred to in a polite way.

1.1) Respectful verbs

The respectful form uses the exact same inflection as the passive form(see lesson 37):

なかむらさんは　７じに　こられます。

Mr. Nakamura is coming at seven.

おさけを　やめられたんですか？

Have you given up drinking?

1.2)おVます-formに　なります

This pattern is considered more polite than the respectful verbs in 1.1. It cannot be used for verbs whose ます-form consists of only one mora(みます, ねます, etc.) and group III verbs.

しゃちょうは　もう　おかえりに　なりました。

The president has already returned home.

#
1.3) Special respectful verbs

Some verbs have special respectful equivalents. They show the same level of respect as 1.2:

どうぞ　めしあがって　ください。

Please help yourselves.

#
1.4) おVます-form　ください

This is the respectful way of instructing or inviting someone to do something.

あちらから　おはいり　ください。

Please enter from over there.

1.4)～まして

In order to be very polite, the て-form can be changed to ～まして:

ハンスが　ゆうべ　ねつを　だしまして、けさも　まだ　さがらないんです。

Hans became feverish last night and it didn't go down this morning.

[Note]

When talking to a close friend about a respected person, the plain form of けいご can be used:

ぶちょうは　なんじに　いらっしゃる？

What time will the general manager come?

#
2) Nouns, adjectives and adverbs

Some nouns, adjectives and adverbs can be turned into そんけいご by attaching お or ご at the front. Basically, お is used for words of Japanese origin and ご for words of Chinese origin

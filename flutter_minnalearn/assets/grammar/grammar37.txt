1.) Passive Verbs

All passive verbs are Group II verbs and conjugate into the dictionary form, ない-form, て-form, etc.

2.) P1は P2に V passive - V was done to P1 by P2

わたしは　せんせいに　ほめられました。　I was praised by my teacher.

わたしは　いぬに　かまれました。　I was bitten by a dog.

3.) P1は P2に Nを V(passive)

This pattern describes an action being done to a person's belongings by another person:

わたしは　おとうとに　パソコンを　こわされました。 My pc was broken by my brother.

This pattern is only used when the person is troubled by the action. If the action is positive, ～て　もらいます is used instead:

わたしは　ともだちに　じてんしゃを　しゅうりして　もらいました。 My bike was repaired by my friend.

4.) N(thing) が/は V(passive)

If the person doing the action doesn't need to be mentioned, the object can become the subject of the sentence:

かいぎは　こうべで　ひらかれました。　The assembly was held in Kobe.

5.) Nは Pに　よって V(passive)

When something is created or discovered, the creator is marked using に　よって:

でんわは　ベルに　よって　はつめいされました。 The phone was invented by Bell.

6.) Nから/Nで　つくります

When something is made from a raw material, the material is marked with から. When it is obvious to the eye that something is made of a particular material, the material is marked with で:

ビールは　むぎから　つくられます。 Beer is made from barley.

いえは　きで　つくられました。　The house was made from wood.

1.) ばあいは  "in case of.."

This expression is used to talk about a hypothetical situation. The sentence after it indicates how to cope with such a situation.

まに　あわない　ばあいは、れんらくして　ください。

If you can't make it on time, please inform me.

じしんの　ばあいは、エレベーターを　つかわないで　ください。

In case of an earthquake, do not use the elevator.

2.) のに "even though.."

This expression is used when what is stated in the second clause runs contrary to what is expected from the first clause.

やくそくを　したのに、きれませんでした。

Even though he promised, he did not come.

きょうは　にちようびなのに、はたらかなければ　なりません。

Even though it is Sunday, I have to work.

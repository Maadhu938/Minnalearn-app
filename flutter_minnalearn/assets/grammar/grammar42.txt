1.1) ために

This sentence pattern indicates a benefit, a purpose, a reason or a cause., as in

"in order to/for/for the benefit of"

じぶんの　みせを　もつ　ために、ちょきんして　います。

In order to have my own store, I'm saving money.

ひっこしの　にもつを　はこぶ　ために、おおきな　くるまを　かります。

I will rent a big car in order to carry the luggage for my move.

けんこうの　ために、まいあさ　はしって　います。

For my health, I run every morning.

かぞくの　ために、うちを　たてます。

I will build a house for my family.

1.2) ～ように (L36)

～ように(learned in lesson 36) is also used to express a purpose, but for sentences that include conditional sense/no volition, as in

"so that.../so as not to..."

にほんごが　じょうずに　なるように、まいにち　べんきょうして　います。

I study every day so that my Japanese may improve.

あしたまでに　とどく　ように、そくたつで　だして　ください。

Please send it by express mail, so that it may arrive until tomorrow.

#
2) に

This pattern is used to indicate a purpose and followed by expressions such as つかう, いい, べんりだ, やくに　たつ, じかんが　かかる.

この　はさみは　はなを　きるのに　つかいます。

This pair of scissors is used for cutting flowers.

この　かばんが　おおきくて、りょこうに　べんり　です。

This bag is big and convenient for trips.

でんわばんごうを　しらべるのに　じかんが　かかりました。

Finding the telephone number took time.

#
3) いきます

This is another way of indicating purpose:

こうべへ　ふねを　みに　いきます。

I'm going to Kobe to look at the ships.

4.) Quantifier は

When attached to a quantifier, は indicates the minimum amount required:

にほんでは　けっこんしきを　するのに　200万円は　いります。

In Japan, you need at least 2 million yen to hold a wedding.

4.) Quantifier も

When attached to a quantifier, も indicates that the speaker thinks the amount mentioned is a lot:

けっこんしきを　するのに　200万円も　いりますか。

You need as much as 2 million yen to hold a wedding?!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MinnaLearn: Japanese N5 & N4 mobile application on Google Play.

MinnaLearn is an interactive Japanese language learning app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with international privacy laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and display name (Firebase Authentication, including Google Sign-In).
  • Learning progress: Lesson completion, study time, streak counters, quiz scores, bookmarked vocabulary, learned kanji, and unlocked achievements.
  • Purchases & Subscriptions: Purchase tokens, transaction receipts, and PRO subscription status processed securely via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. We never collect or store your payment card details.
  • App Diagnostics & Analytics: Aggregate screen views, session duration, and feature engagement via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) collected by Google AdMob strictly for delivering rewarded and interstitial advertisements. In the EEA and UK, ad personalization is governed by your consent settings.
  • Approximate location: Coarse city/region inferred by Firebase from IP for CDN latency optimization; no GPS or real-time location is collected.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide, operate, and maintain your account and learning data.
  • Sync your study progress across multiple Android devices when signed in.
  • Manage PRO entitlements, subscription renewals, and purchase restorations via RevenueCat.
  • Serve optional rewarded video ads (allowing free users to unlock lessons) and interstitial ads via Google AdMob.
  • Send local study reminders and streak notifications (only when explicitly permitted).
  • Diagnose bugs, detect crashes, and improve overall app stability.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We partner with reputable service providers who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore (cloud database sync), and Firebase Analytics.
  • Google AdMob: Ad serving, rewarded ads, and consent management via Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Governed by Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription infrastructure and in-app purchase validation. Governed by RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Payment processing for digital purchases and subscriptions.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your study progress is saved locally on your device (SQLite) and encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS when synced to Cloud Firestore.
  • Google Firebase data centers maintain industry-leading physical, technical, and organizational security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3).

5. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • EEA & UK Users (GDPR): You have the right to access, rectify, port, or erase your data. Users in the EEA and UK are presented with a Google UMP consent dialog to accept or reject personalized advertising. You may change your consent preferences at any time in app settings.
  • California Residents (CCPA): We do not "sell" personal information as defined by the CCPA. You have the right to know what personal data is collected and request its deletion.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

  • Access & Correction: View and update your profile details within the app.
  • Instant Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the app (Profile -> Account Settings -> Delete Account) or by submitting a request on our Data Deletion Page.
  • Sign Out / Offline Mode: You can study 100% offline as a guest without creating an account.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MinnaLearn is designed for high school students, university learners, and adults aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
